What Makes Block PLANDH Unique?

The uniqueness is not simply the integration of railway data. The innovation is how Block PLANDH converts operational information into actionable maintenance opportunities.

From Block Allocation → Opportunity Discovery

Traditional maintenance planning generally starts with a maintenance requirement and then manually searches for a suitable block.

Block PLANDH reverses this process. It analyses maintenance priority, work duration, train timetable, current train movement, route capacity, station information and operational constraints to discover feasible maintenance windows.

We don't simply decide “which block to give.”
We intelligently discover “where the railway has an opportunity to perform maintenance.”

Theory Explanation

Block PLANDH transforms railway maintenance planning from a static and manual scheduling process into a dynamic, opportunity-driven decision-support system.

The system combines maintenance priority, work duration, train timetable, current train movement, route capacity, station information and operational constraints to identify feasible maintenance windows.

Instead of simply allocating a predefined block, Block PLANDH discovers suitable opportunities between train movements and evaluates whether a maintenance activity can practically fit within that window.

The system can support planned, date-based and emergency maintenance requirements while identifying potential operational conflicts before the proposed schedule reaches the approval stage.

Most importantly, Block PLANDH follows a Human-in-the-Loop approach. AI provides intelligent recommendations and explanations, while the Control Office and Station Master retain responsibility for reviewing and approving the final operational plan.
